Space — Privacy policy Updated 10 October 2026. Developer: Xekep (Space project). Space is an offline game. It has no account registration, advertisements or analytics SDK. The game does not transmit your worlds, scores or device sensor readings to the developer. On your device it stores language, sound and control settings, scores, completed challenges, three manual world slots and a separate session recovery file. Recovery is written periodically and when pausing; the latest completed snapshot can be restored after interruption. Files remain until overwritten or you clear app storage or uninstall. Clearing storage removes worlds and progress. Android may back up and restore application data using your system backup settings and provider; the developer does not operate that backup service. When enabled, accelerometer/motion sensors provide tilt steering or shaking. Readings are processed in memory for gameplay, are not written as a sensor history, and stop when the application is paused. Vibration produces local feedback. World import and export use the Android document picker. You choose the document and destination; exported JSON contains the world's name, positions, velocities, masses, routes and camera state. Sharing that file is your choice and is governed by the receiving service. World names may contain text you entered: avoid including private information in files you share. Support opens GitHub in your browser. GitHub handles any account or information you submit under its own privacy terms. Do not post private information or credentials in public issues. If you contact support, the information you voluntarily provide is used to answer the request; request deletion of your message through the support channel and the service where it was posted. Store purchase, payment and diagnostic data handled by Google Play or RuStore follow the respective store policies; Space does not receive your payment-card details. Contact and privacy inquiries: https://github.com/Xekep/Space/issues
